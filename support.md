# Support – دەرڤە (Derve)

Derve is a party game in Badini Kurdish for 3 to 12 players on one phone.
There is no account, no purchase and nothing to set up, so most questions
are about how to play.

## Contact
Found a bug, a wrong word or photo, or have a suggestion?
[Send a message here](https://github.com/cloudyonthemic/derve-privacy/issues/new)
(a free GitHub account is needed). Please mention your phone model and the
app version, shown at the bottom of the settings screen.

## Common questions

**How do I play?**
Tap the question mark on the home screen to see the rules in four steps.

**How many players do I need?**
Between 3 and 12. Use the + and − buttons at the top of the home screen.

**How do I change player names?**
Tap the people icon on the home screen.

**How do I reset the scores or change the winning score?**
Open the settings with the gear icon on the home screen.

**There is no sound.**
Check that sound is on in the settings, and that the phone's silent switch
is off. The game follows the silent switch.

**Does the game need the internet?**
No. It works fully offline and sends nothing anywhere.

**How do I delete my data?**
Names and scores are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app removes
them.

## Privacy
The game collects no data. Read the [privacy policy](./).
